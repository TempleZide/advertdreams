# Prototype: product splash

Throwaway marketing page for the advertdreams shape. Not production. Not a live signup.

Open `index.html` or serve the folder:

```sh
python3 -m http.server 4173 --directory prototypes/splash
```

Then visit `http://localhost:4173`.

The three ads and the job photographs come from [`prototype/ad-creative`](https://github.com/TempleZide/advertdreams/tree/prototype/ad-creative) — Appalachian Excavating & Grading, Lenoir NC. Copy follows `CONTEXT.md` and the standing constraints on [map #2](https://github.com/TempleZide/advertdreams/issues/2). Tier prices are not shown; they are still an open grilling ticket.

## Read this before showing it to anyone

The page asserts **"ad spend included, you never get a second bill from Meta"** as settled. It is not. [#25](https://github.com/TempleZide/advertdreams/issues/25) found the market bears roughly $149–249 per month while a civil construction Client needs $1,000–1,500 per month of Ad Spend to escape Meta's learning phase — the two cannot both hold, and the open question is whether the Client's own card gets attached to an ad account advertdreams creates and administers. Until that is decided, treat that line as a claim under review rather than a promise to a real contractor.

Everything else on the page follows a standing constraint on [map #2](https://github.com/TempleZide/advertdreams/issues/2): subscription only with no per-lead fee, the Client's Facebook Page stays theirs, Creatives composited over the Client's own photographs, and a queued Creative that auto-publishes after 48 hours unless killed.
