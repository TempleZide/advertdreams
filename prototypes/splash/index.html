How it works

You already have the proof. It’s on your phone.

Agencies sell the same bundle at six to eight hundred a month before anyone spends a dollar on ads. You pay one subscription. We fund the Campaign. You keep the jobs.

  1. 01

    Intake

    Services, service area, and photos off your phone. We walk you through sharing your Facebook Page. Nothing transfers. Setup finishes in the background.

  2. 02

    Creatives

    Ads are composited over your own job photos. We do not invent a driveway you did not build. You have 48 hours to kill anything you don’t like.

  3. 03

    Campaign

    Live on Facebook from your Page, aimed at your service area. Ad spend sits inside the subscription. You never get a second bill from Meta.

  4. 04

    Leads

    Instant form, a form on your Site, a call or a text to a Tracking Number that forwards to you. One list. A ping on your phone.

Leads

The job shows up as a text.

A Lead is a person who asked for the work — form, call, or SMS — attributed to the Campaign that produced them. Dashboard, plus a text and an email to the owner. No per-lead fee, so the list stays honest.

The bundle

One subscription. The whole stack.

A Site people land on, Creatives from your media, Campaigns we fund, a Tracking Number that forwards to you, and Leads in one place.

Site

One styled page with your services, service area, photos, form, and number. Chosen from a small set of ready-made looks.

Creatives

Ads rendered from your photographs and videos. Several per Service so you can choose and the platform can pick a winner.

Campaign

Targeting, budget, and the ads running against it. Ad spend is a cost we carry, capped inside your tier.

Tracking Number

A number we own, assigned to you, forwarded to your real phone. Calls and texts count as Leads.

Dashboard

The list, the photos, the kill switch on a queued Creative. Plus a text and an email the moment a Lead lands.

What you do

  • Send phone-original photos of finished work
  • Share your Facebook Page, not the keys to it
  • Kill a Creative in one tap if a claim is wrong
  • Answer the phone when a Lead comes in

What you don’t

  • Learn Ads Manager
  • Pay an agency, then pay Meta on top
  • Argue about what counts as a Lead
  • Hand over the Page you already built
